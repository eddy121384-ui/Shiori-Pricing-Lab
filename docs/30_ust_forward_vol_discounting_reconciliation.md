### Calculation-path findings, not name matching

* The Annex route starts from spot clean, calls the bond accrual helper at valuation and expiry, obtains coupon flows in `(valuation_date, expiry_date]`, discounts every coupon and expiry using only the selected Bond Reference Curve, divides spot dirty less coupon PV by the expiry DF, and subtracts expiry AI. The curve purpose is hard-coded, not caller-selectable.
* The browser route is a different reviewed path. It cannot call that construction because its standalone guard requires an explicit `BLIForwardCleanPriceInput` and requires only the Option Discount Curve. Its bond quote is not substituted for the explicit forward.
* `EQUIVALENT_PRICE_VOL` is a declaration that the supplied number is already price sigma. It is not evidence that Annex A.8 converted `YIELD_VOL`; neither MODE_1 nor MODE_2 symbols, inputs, calculations, or reporting exist in the executable path.
* The standalone discount ratio is computed before Black-76 and passed as the sole DF. Therefore it discounts the option payoff to option settlement and then expresses it at reporting date. Expiry affects option time, not this DF target. The older bundle path discounts to expiry instead.

### Confirmed alignment

1. The Annex forward primitive itself matches every A.5 arithmetic component and keeps Bond Reference Curve separate.
2. Direct decimal annual `PRICE_VOL` and already-converted `EQUIVALENT_PRICE_VOL` have a deterministic Black-76 input contract; `YIELD_VOL` is rejected rather than silently consumed.
3. Benchmark calibration targets premium per 100, not Price (%) or total premium, and preserves side/source/timestamp identity gates.
4. Curve purpose and rate basis are independent typed fields. Interpolation and continuous discounting are deterministic and do not bootstrap, extrapolate, or guess Bloomberg conventions.
5. Valuation date, expiry timestamp, reporting date, forward settlement date, and option settlement date are explicit and are not derived from settlement lag.

### Gaps by kind

**Data routes missing**

* approved standalone Annex-forward inputs/Bond Reference Curve, or a proven acquisition/transcription route for the explicit OVME clean forward;
* approved S490-to-`CONTINUOUS_ZERO_RATE` transformation and source metadata;
* approved OVME benchmark premium capture route (including exact per-100 unit and side);
* any approved yield-vol/proxy-swaption source and its tenor, strike/moneyness, basis, side, and conversion inputs.

**Implementation gaps (relative to SPEC/Annex authority)**

* Annex A.8 MODE_1 and MODE_2, mode selection, validation, provenance, and reporting;
* standalone Annex A.5 forward construction (the separate bundle implementation exists, but is not wired to the browser route);
* complete export of curve node inputs and all separate date/DF roles needed for independent reconciliation.

**`OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`**

1. Whether a repo-based forward may replace, coexist with, or remain evidence-only against Annex A's Bond Reference Curve approximation.
2. Whether yield-vol conversion is formally enabled, and whether any OVME proxy swaption yield vol is an approved source.
3. Whether UST standalone option discounting is to expiry or to option settlement and then reporting date.
4. Any Bloomberg field whose unit, basis, side, compounding, interpolation, or date role cannot be proven.

## 3. Minimum OVME evidence specification

Capture **two UST cases** with different coupon timing relative to expiry: (A) no coupon in `(valuation date, expiry]`; (B) at least one coupon in that interval. Both must be fixed-coupon bullets, European, price payoff. Use the same explicit quote side within each case. No Gilt, full-screen transcription, terminal identifier, credentials, or raw proprietary payload is requested.

The known sanitized OVME golden case in `tests/test_bli_ovme_golden_case.py` is a useful **Case A software baseline**, because it pins explicit forward, direct price vol, premium display bucket, dates, and discount factors. It is explicitly anonymized/synthetic and is **not sufficient external evidence**: it lacks a retained human capture proving the original labels/side/date roles, and it cannot test pre-expiry coupon treatment. Use it to choose a comparable Case A shape, not as a second Bloomberg observation.

### Capture workflow and stopping rule

1. Populate `docs/evidence/ust_ovme_capture_template.json` twice, leaving unavailable fields `null` and recording a status; never invent a value.
2. Keep screenshots and raw Bloomberg exports outside Git. Commit only an approved redacted transcription or hashes/references that reveal no terminal/user identifier.
3. For Case A, compare direct `PRICE_VOL`, unit premium, explicit forward, and discount dates. For Case B, additionally reconcile the coupon-PV boundary.
4. A missing individual field does not invalidate the whole capture: mark only the affected comparison `UNRESOLVED`. Do not use plausible output agreement as mapping evidence.
5. Stop for owner decision at every repo-vs-Bond-Reference, yield-vol conversion, expiry-vs-settlement discount, or unproved Bloomberg-semantics conflict.

### Deterministic replay requirements

A capture is replay-ready only when all of the following are present and mutually coherent:

1. complete fixed-coupon schedule terms, including `issue_date` (dated date), first/last coupon dates, maturity, frequency and day-count;
2. the exact normalized standalone request used for the run, referenced by `normalized_request_sha256` after redaction/licensing review;
3. `pricing_status`, product/snapshot IDs, valuation/pricing timestamps, market-data source/as-of/retrieved-at, pricing engine name/version, method, request contract name and producing Git commit;
4. Shiori model fair premium per 100 and unsigned total-notional model fair premium, plus the explicit `position_sign_applied_to_shiori_model_premium = false` contract; and
5. the redacted current-run export referenced by `current_run_export_sha256`.

DAPI/reference data supplies eligible bond terms and routed market observations; the deterministic Shiori run supplies calculation outputs, status, engine/method and run provenance; manual OVME capture supplies only the explicitly listed OVME screen evidence. A hash binds an approved redacted artifact—it does not make an uncommittable raw Bloomberg payload safe, and it does not repair a missing field.
