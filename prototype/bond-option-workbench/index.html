Bond reference — profile-filled, trader-editable
For a supported UST fixed-coupon bullet, Shiori fills these from the approved narrow UST profile and its own deterministic derivations, and shows the source of every value. Each field is resolved on its own: one field Shiori cannot resolve is marked BLOCKED for you to set and never blanks the others. Bloomberg’s DAY_CNT_DES, MTY_TYP and CALC_TYP_DES remain display-only evidence and are still never coerced into a Shiori enum — MTY_TYP and CALC_TYP_DES decide nothing at all (a real UST returns NORMAL), and a contradicting DAY_CNT_DES can only withhold the day count itself. A bond whose product or coupon schedule this pricing path cannot carry gets no fields and these controls are disabled, because editing them could not make it price. Otherwise every control stays editable; the moment you change one, its source becomes TRADER_OVERRIDE and Shiori stops re-deriving it.

Timing & settlement — profile-filled, trader-editable
Still no Bloomberg field describes an OTC option’s own cash settlement date, and SETTLE_DT / DAYS_TO_SETTLE describe the cash bond’s standard settlement — a different role that must not be conflated. For a supported UST, Shiori derives these itself instead: Reporting Date is the run’s own Valuation Date, and both settlement dates are the expiry rolled forward one U.S. government-bond business day on the existing QuantLib U.S. government-bond calendar — no hand-written holiday table anywhere. Change the expiry and the settlement dates you have not overridden are re-derived; change one yourself and Shiori leaves it alone.

Option Discount Curve — trader override
Nodes are recorded as OPTION_DISCOUNT_CURVE / CONTINUOUS_ZERO_RATE in —. Tenors use the existing grammar only: nD, nM, nY. Enter genuine continuously-compounded zero rates. Bloomberg OVME’s MMkt and repo rates, and any par/swap/FTP rate, are not continuous zero rates and must not be entered here. Shiori does not bootstrap, convert, or extrapolate a curve; rates are interpolated in-range only.
