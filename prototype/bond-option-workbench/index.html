    Valuation, Timing & Settlement
    Collapse
    Timestamps accept an explicit offset (e.g. 2026-07-20T11:28:00+08:00) and are sent exactly as entered. Pricing Timestamp and Expiry Timestamp are matched against Valuation Date and Expiry Date by the local calendar date they represent, so 2026-10-20T05:20:00+08:00 belongs to Expiry Date 2026-10-20. As-Of Timestamp is the one value respelled in UTC at the contract boundary, because its no-look-ahead check works on the UTC date. The UTC equivalent shown beneath each field is for transparency only and never changes what is sent. Calendar dates are independent explicit inputs: Shiori never infers a timezone, market close, holiday, business-day adjustment, or a settlement date from them.

    Bond Reference Completion
    Collapse
    Bloomberg confirms coupon, frequency, issue/maturity/first-coupon dates and the callable/sinkable flags. The fields below have no confirmed mnemonic (REDEMPTION_VALUE and PENULTIMATE_COUPON_DATE both returned BAD_FLD), so the trader supplies them. Where Bloomberg returns a raw description it is shown beneath the field as a hint only — Shiori never maps DAY_CNT_DES, MTY_TYP or CALC_TYP_DES into a typed enum.

    Option Discount Curve
    Collapse
    Nodes are recorded as OPTION_DISCOUNT_CURVE / CONTINUOUS_ZERO_RATE in —. Tenors use the existing grammar only: nD, nM, nY. Enter genuine continuously-compounded zero rates. Bloomberg OVME’s MMkt and repo rates, and any par/swap/FTP rate, are not continuous zero rates and must not be entered here. Shiori does not bootstrap, convert, or extrapolate a curve; rates are interpolated in-range only.
